Before you ask

A few practical questions.

Can you build my first business website?

Yes. You don’t need an existing site. We start with your services, coverage area, and contact details, then plan the pages and help organize the content.

Can you improve the website I already have?

Yes. Send me the URL and tell me what isn’t working for you. I’ll review it before recommending targeted updates or a rebuild.

Will I be able to update it myself?

If you want to edit your own pages, we’ll choose a platform that supports that and include an editing handoff in the scope. We decide this before the build starts.

How much does it cost, and how long does it take?

Both depend on the pages, features, and content needed. After our first conversation, you’ll get a quote and proposed timeline before you commit. Revision rounds and responsibilities are agreed upfront.

Does a website build include ongoing SEO?

The build includes the agreed SEO setup for your pages. Ongoing local SEO, new content, and maintenance are separate services. We can plan those together if you need them.

Do you only work with businesses in Florence?

I’m based in Florence and focus on home service businesses across the Shoals, including Muscle Shoals, Sheffield, Tuscumbia, and nearby communities. If you’re elsewhere, tell me about your project.
