Wesley Hopkins

Founder, Crawled · Digital Visibility Architect

I'm Wesley Hopkins, founder of Crawled. I've spent most of my career trying to understand what makes information visible online: how search works beneath the surface, how discovery happens, and why it all matters. That's also where the name "Crawled" comes from — crawling is how discovery begins, how Google finds new pages, and how everything online connects.

Crawled exists to help businesses build authority across both traditional search and the AI systems that increasingly decide what gets cited and recommended. Alongside SEO and GEO work, I also draw on a background in project management and digital reputation.

On the client side, I built the content architecture and wrote the topical cluster for the HVAC/R AI Pro case study — taking a brand new domain with no search history to page-one rankings and a first-place mention in Google's AI Overview.

You can read more of my thinking on search, SEO, and GEO on the Crawled blog, or get in touch if you'd like to talk about your own visibility.
