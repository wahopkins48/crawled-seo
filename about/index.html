About Crawled

SEO + GEO agency for how search actually works now

Crawled helps businesses build authority across both traditional search and the AI systems that increasingly decide what gets cited and recommended. The name comes from crawling — how discovery begins, how Google finds new pages, and how everything online connects.

On the client side, that approach built the content architecture and topical cluster behind the HVAC/R AI Pro case study — taking a brand new domain with no search history to page-one rankings and a first-place mention in Google's AI Overview.

The person behind it

Crawled is a one-person shop, run by me — Wesley Hopkins. I've spent most of my career trying to understand what makes information visible online: how search works beneath the surface, how discovery happens, and why it all matters. Alongside SEO and GEO work, I draw on a background in project management and digital reputation, and I'm HubSpot SEO Certified — though I stay hands-on with how search algorithms and AI ranking systems actually behave rather than relying on certifications alone.

You can read more of my thinking on search, SEO, and GEO on the Crawled blog, or get in touch if you'd like to talk about your own visibility.
