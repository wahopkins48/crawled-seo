| Plan | Monthly price | One-time setup | Commitment |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |

| Growth | $1,250 | $200 | Month to month |
| Authority | $1,850 | $200 | Month to month |

Keep the public plan descriptions and comparison table aligned with the current pricing guide before publishing future changes.
