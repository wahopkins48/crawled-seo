Terms summary

Good to know

Billing

Billed monthly by invoice and paid in advance. Prices are per location. Additional locations are quoted separately.

Setup fee

A one-time $200 setup fee applies to every plan. It covers the cleanup of your Google profile, listings, and website basics, is due at the start, and is non-refundable.

Commitment

Local Starter has a three-month minimum. Other plans are month to month. Cancellations take effect at the end of the billing month.

Timeline

Some local improvements show up in 30 to 60 days. Meaningful growth usually takes three to six months.

Town and service pages

New pages are added until your towns and services are covered. After that, that time goes to improving and updating your existing pages, so your site never fills up with near-duplicate pages that only swap the town name.

No guaranteed rankings

Google and AI companies control their results, so no honest provider guarantees rankings. You get white-hat work and clear reporting.

What Wesley needs from you

Manager access to your Google Business Profile, website editing access, accurate business details, and photos of your work.
