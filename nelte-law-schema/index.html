Where it goes

  1. In Strikingly, open Settings, then Advanced, then Custom Code (some accounts label it Head Code).
  2. Paste the block above into the head section and publish.
  3. Check it with Google's Rich Results Test. Paste in your homepage URL and confirm it detects the law firm and the attorney.

One thing to confirm first

The address, phone number, and hours need to match your Google Business Profile exactly, character for character. If the two disagree, correct whichever is out of date before publishing this, because a mismatch weakens the connection rather than strengthening it.
