What does Crawled actually do?

Crawled helps businesses get found online. We improve your visibility in:

  • Google Search & Google Business Profile
  • AI Overviews
  • Generative engines like ChatGPT and Perplexity
  • Local map results & High-intent search queries

We focus on structured visibility, entity clarity, and machine-readable signals so search engines and AI systems understand who you are and when to recommend you.

What’s the difference between SEO and GEO?

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) helps your website rank in traditional search results. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) prepares your brand to be referenced inside AI-generated answers.

While most agencies still optimize for blue links, Crawled optimizes for AI summaries, entity-based search, knowledge signals, and structured authority.

Do you guarantee rankings?

No ethical SEO agency can guarantee rankings because Google controls its algorithm and AI systems evolve constantly. We guarantee strategic, white-hat implementation, transparent reporting, and clear monthly deliverables.

How long does it take and do I need a contract?

SEO is compounding. Some local improvements appear within 30–60 days, but meaningful growth occurs over 3–6 months. Strong authority often takes 6–12 months.

We typically recommend a 3–6 month minimum to allow for consistency, but we believe retention comes from results, not restrictive contracts.

What industries do you specialize in?

We work best with home services (HVAC, renovation, roofing), professional services (law, consulting, accounting), healthcare-adjacent providers, and authority-driven personal brands.

Do you sell backlinks?

We build links strategically based on relevance, context, and topical reinforcement. We do not sell spammy link packages because links without proper site structure are just noise.

Can I do SEO myself?

Yes. However, most business owners don't have the time to stay current on algorithm changes or manage the ongoing signals required for entity-based search. We handle the management so you can run your business.
