Privacy

How this website uses information.

Updated September 25, 2026

Inquiries and replies

When you submit a form, the details you enter are sent to Formspree so Wesley Hopkins can review and respond to your request. These may include your name, contact details, business name, website, town, and message. Do not send passwords, private account access, or payment details.

Formspree processes submissions. Wesley uses business email to communicate about inquiries and services. A form-service receipt confirms acceptance of a request; it does not prove an email notification reached an inbox.

An inquiry does not subscribe you to a newsletter or a marketing sequence.

Campaign information

This site remembers an approved campaign label and landing-page path in your browser tab to help identify whether an inquiry came through a known flyer, video, or business-card link. It sends those labels, the landing path, and the submission path with the inquiry. It does not store form contents in browser storage.

Unrecognized campaign values, raw URL queries, and fragments are omitted from this metadata. If your browser blocks session storage, attribution is limited to the current document. A recognized campaign label does not prove a QR code was scanned. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals suppress campaign storage and optional analytics.

Optional analytics

Google Analytics is off until you choose to allow it using the control below or in the footer. Your choice is stored in this browser when storage is available. You can turn it off again. Forms work either way.

When enabled, Google Analytics receives page-view information and events for accepted inquiries, telephone-link clicks, and booking-link clicks. Events include approved page paths, audience labels, form names, and approved campaign labels. Submitted names, email addresses, phone numbers, website fields, and messages are not included in these events.

Google may process technical information, including browser and network information, and use cookies when analytics is enabled. The site currently sends to two existing Google Analytics properties while the reporting setup is reviewed. Telephone and booking clicks do not confirm a call or appointment.

Other services and links

Vercel hosts the website. Google Fonts supplies the typefaces and receives the network information needed to serve them. Booking links open Calendly, where its privacy practices apply. External services may process technical information necessary to provide their services. These inquiry forms do not collect payment information.

Questions and requests

Contact wesley@crawledseo.com to ask about information you have shared, or request access, correction, or deletion. Do not include sensitive information in a public website URL.

No method of transmission is completely secure. This page will be updated when the site's data practices change.
